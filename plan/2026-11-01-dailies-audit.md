# Plan, 1 Nov: audit Dailies for function, design and ease of use

The boss: "audit the app for functional, design and user friendliness".
The judging criteria are not found yet; the boss has no Safari, so the
phone test will be Chrome on Android.

## How
- Walk the live app the way a judge would, in Chrome for Testing: the home
  page, /make/ and each tool (link, store, long video, "Put yourself in
  it", planner, the key page), the bio page, privacy, the sign-in, at 390
  and 1280 px, light and dark. Click every control, read every message,
  try the wrong input (a bad link, a huge file, nothing).
- Three lists: broken (doesn't work or misleads), design (looks wrong or
  unprofessional), ease (a stranger would stall). Each item says where,
  what, and the fix.
- Fix what is small on the spot; plan the rest in order of what a judge
  would hit first. Report to the boss with screenshots of the worst.

## Out
- New features. The boss's own phone run, which only they can do.
